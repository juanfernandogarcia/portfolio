Bioethanol Distillation Retrofit

A 40 kt/y bioethanol purification train ran two columns in series with no heat recovery. Steam accounted for 61% of variable cost, and the plant wanted the saving inside a ten-day shutdown.

Role
Sole author
Period

Tools

02Extracted stream data and built composite curves; pinch located at ΔTmin = 10 °C.
03Placed two exchangers above the pinch, sized them and costed the retrofit against a nine-day turnaround.
